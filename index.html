Common questions

Quick answers before you call.

How much does a borewell cost?

It depends on the depth, the ground and the current diesel price. Call or WhatsApp us for today’s rate. We tell you the cost before work starts.

How deep will my borewell go?

It depends on the ground and the water level in your area. Tell us your village and we will advise you.

Can you repair an old borewell?

Yes. We do air flushing, re-cutting and deepening for old or choked bores.

Which areas do you serve?

Piduguralla, Karempudi and nearby towns and villages. Ask us if you are not sure about your area.
